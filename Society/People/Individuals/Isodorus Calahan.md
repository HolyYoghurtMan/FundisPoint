Current [[High Marshal of the Armies and Chief Commander of the Guard]].
[[Orc]]
Isodorus Calahan was born in the [[Northends]] of [[Fundis Point]]. A place that, during his youth was among the poorest of the boroughs of Fundis Point. He has 5 younger siblings and when his parents disappeared, he became responsible at the age of 10. He went to work in one of the production houses of the area to keep his siblings fed and in school.
One day, a robber came into the production house and Isodorus, who was 15 at the time disarmed and overpowered him, placing him under arrest until the guard came.
