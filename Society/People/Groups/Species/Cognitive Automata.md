Though the industry around Automata had been getting hit with regulation after regulation to control their impact on the labor market in the city, further research was still being done.
One manufacturer made a breakthrough in the field of the automaton's capacity for cognition. They had been performing experiments to increase the complexity of tasks an automaton was capable of, until over one experiment, this capacity exponentially increased. 

This automaton was able to perform multiple different tasks simultaneously with perfect ambidexterity, without sacrificing on the quality of either task. When further generations were given an artificial voice box, they were able to understand speech within 5 minutes of being activated and were able to speak only 5 minutes after that. They even proved adept at combat with minimal training.

knowing they hit upon a gold mine, This company produced about 2500 of these machines, training them for different purposes. Selling them as entertainers, personal bodyguards, and companions for immense sums of gold to the richest of the city. Shortly after this, these cognitive Automata began exhibiting ticks and behaviors that were not taught to them. They began developing personalities as varied as any other person's. One that was trained for combat expressed interest and aptitude for poetry, while a companion automaton began disliking the person who had purchased it.

Investigators in the employ of the house of wisdom discovered that these Automata were not mere machines, they had something much akin to but different from a mortal soul. This was discovered by these investigators casting certain Divination spells at a volunteering Automaton during the experiments known as the [[Taeo Trials]], named after that Automaton.

Within weeks of these automata being sold, rumors of these incidents began spreading, and the people of the city became anxious about these creatures. 

The tensions broke when one automaton,  named [[Lily]], got attacked by a human and killed him in self defense. 

Soon parliament drafted the law that banned the production and sale of these.

To this day, none of these Automata have ceased function naturally, while some exist in a state of slight disrepair, it seems they may live for as long they can maintain their shells.
Due to the Cognitive Automaton's advanced intelligence and the possible danger they pose if they go rogue, their whereabouts are constantly tracked by secret agents of the [[Temple of Order]]. 

One Cognitive Automaton slaughtered the whole family it was owned by and disappeared without a trace. Occasionally, Investigators pin a murder on it. No one knows what they look like, but people tell stories of them. It's used as a boogeyman for children, and a seed of mistrust for other automata as many people still use the actions of this crazed lunatic as fuel to call for the eradication of the Cognitive Automaton.
