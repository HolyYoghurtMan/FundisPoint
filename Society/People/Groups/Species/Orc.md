A green skinned kind of mortal. The average orc is tall and powerfully built, with large bottom tusks jutting from their mouth.

What records remain of before the Breach, indicate a fraught relationship with humans[^1]. At the time of the breach, the orcs lived as nomads. Their tribes reached thousands and spread far and wide throughout a region they passed through. Staying in a location for months until the time came to move on.

[^1]: A theologist's account indicates that the creators of Humans and Orcs were like bickering siblings and they created these species of mortal in a competition. [[creation of the Orc and Human]]
