The Guild of Civil Watchmen is the organization created by different mercenary companies in the city.

The Guild of Free Swords is in [[The Guildsleague]]. Their [[Hall of Blades|Guildhouse]] stands tall at the edge of the [[Blade Wharf]]

Any mercenary company in the city must be acknowledged by the Guild before being allowed to operate normally. The Guild asks on average for 1/4th of a member company's income, collected every month. 

If a company is acknowledged by the Guild, they receive a number of benefits:
- They will be recommended to an interested party
- They will be allowed to charge more (on average half more)
- They will be provided legal representation if necessary

However some conditions apply to being allowed membership:
- A Member company's Chief must vouch for you
- All of a company's soldiers must undergo certification tests, proving their physical fitness and skill at arms to the Guild and the City Watch

The following penalties apply to an unacknowledged company:
- If the Guild discovers the company has done work without their know, the Guild will note half that job's pay as fees. Any outstanding fees must be paid before a company can be considered for membership.
- Not formally, but clients will not be keen to pay even half of the normal Guild price if a company is not a member.
- If a company is found to lie about membership, they will not be allowed membership in the future. The individual soldiers of the company will be kept track of, and if they join another non-member company, that company will be refused entry so long as that individual is in their ranks.
