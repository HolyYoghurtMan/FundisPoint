A colloquial term for the private mercenaries that work in the [[The Tunnels]] of the Blade Wharf.

There's about X companies that only work in the Tunnels, though they work together very closely. They have agreements over which areas are the responsibilities of which company, and these agreements are absolute. In the past, before the different companies came to some sort of peace, they often had brawls in the tunnels, disregarding the agreements can often cause brawls again.

They are very protective of the function they serve and will oppose outsiders taking security jobs in the tunnels. Given that the scab is licensed by the [[Guild of Free Swords]], they have a 1 strike system for this infringement, do it once and you'll be warned. Do it twice and you'll get punched once by a person for every company that is part of the Tunnelmen. A popular drinking song tells the story of an outsider being caught scabbing twice and the X[^1] people who get to punch him.

The Tunnelmen companies all have a heraldic badger on a brown field in a quarter of their insignia. Any Tunnelman can easily tell another apart because of it.

[^1]: the official amount of companies in the tunnelman, as yet undecided
