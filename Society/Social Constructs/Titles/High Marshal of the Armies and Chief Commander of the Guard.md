A lengthy title for a foundational role in the safety of the city, though the citizenry doesn't know exactly how foundational. 

The title consists of 2 distinct parts: High Marshal and Chief Commander, and performs those two functions. As High Marshal, they are the topmost authority for all troops manning the walls of the city, while the Chief Commander part presides over the policing of the citizens.

The titles are currently held by [[Isodorus Calahan]], who has held them for 20 years.
