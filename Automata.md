A catch-all term for machines that operate on their own. Most Automata are not capable of complex thought, being built entirely for a singular simple task, loading and unloading carriages, filling crates and carrying those. 
Though the knowledge of how to make them was kept alive in the journey to Fundis Point, the construction of new ones is heavily regulated, and in most cases forbidden, as a way to secure the labor market in the city. This came as a result of the successful [[54 FT Labor Riot]], which led to the draft of the [[Bill on the Regulation of Automaton Construction]].
In the years following the Founding of Fundis Point, one Automaton manufacturer made a breakthrough in the cognitive capacities of the Automaton, creating them for tasks such as security and personal assistance. [[Cognitive Automata|These]] became very popular with the elite of the city, about 2500 were made before production was [[Order for  the cessation on Production of Cognitive Automata|cancelled]].
