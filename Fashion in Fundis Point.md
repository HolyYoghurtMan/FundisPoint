Due to its far northern location, [[Fundis Point]] has a cold climate. The city is covered in snow for most of the year, sometimes less and often more than less. From the middle of spring to the end of summer, temperatures may rise up to 20 degrees.

Fashion reflects the cold climate and the brutalist temperament of the general populace. Garments are generally thick but simple and sleek. Heavy woolen coats with fur linings and fur cloaks are commonplace in winter.
