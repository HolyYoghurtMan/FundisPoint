A city built upon the slopes of a lonely mountain in the middle of the [[Carrion Tundra]]. The oldest parts of the city predate the [[Year of the Breach]], but no record survives of its true name, nor of the mountain.

There are [[Eight Great Temples]] throughout the city, not only dedicated to one of the [[Nine Gods]] but told to be a direct gift from them.

The city lies far north of the planet's equator. During winter, the sun only shines for 4 hours. Temperatures can fall very far.
