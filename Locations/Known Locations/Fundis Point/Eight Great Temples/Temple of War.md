Also referred to as the Citadel.

An enormous fortress that stands on the Southernmost point of the city, built from a stone that leaves no seam and offers no weakness. 

This structure has a thin hexagonal footprint, and its sheer walls rise over a hundred meters high. Atop the structure, the citizens of the city have built siege engines, and impossible lifts connect the [[Blade Wharf]] to that place.

The Temple is made of the same contiguous stone that [[The Wall of Fundis]]' foundations were made from.

The massive gates to the hall at the bottom floor can be magically locked to be completely immovable to anyone who does not hold the [[Ward Key Signet]]. This has always been the [[High Marshal of the Armies and Chief Commander of the Guard]]. Should the city be invaded, only they have the authority and ability to lock this bastion to the outside.
