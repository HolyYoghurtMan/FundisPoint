The Nine Great Temples of [[Fundis Point]] are gifts from [[The Nameless 8]] They were beacons that gave their followers safety when the [[Year of the Breach]] became dire. Though the names of [[Nine Gods]] are lost to time, the people of [[Fundis Point]] still worship at these Temples and pray to gods they don't remember.

The Eight temples are enormous structures.

The 8 Temples are:
- [[Temple of Bounty]]
- [[Temple of Ash]]
- [[Temple of Order]]
- [[Temple of War]]
- [[Temple of Health]]
- [[Temple of Wisdom]]
- [[Temple of Stars]]
