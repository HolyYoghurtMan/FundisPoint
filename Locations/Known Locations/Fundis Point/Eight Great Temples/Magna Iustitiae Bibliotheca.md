The section in the [[Temple of Order]] where the laws of Fundis Point and all cases of trial since record keeping began after Article 382 went into effect.

The books detailing the constitution and the amendments are kept on a high pedestal in the middle of the vault, around which an opening to the lower floors creates a moat, only crossed by a single bridge no more than 2 meters wide. At the end of that bridge, two knights stand day and night, relieved every 8 hours.
