A pest that occurs frequently in places where large amounts of coin is stored. Outside of a vault the Hoard Scarab looks like a common beetle, but once it infiltrates a place where coin is stored, it quickly creates plating on its shields to begin replicating the look of the coin. It does this by consuming the coin, and then like hair growth on humanoids, the shield starts building on itself and expanding.

They create large heaps as their nests, from the processed metals they consume. Sometimes these heaps become a large [[Hoard Scarab Nest Golem]].
