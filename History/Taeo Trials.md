A series of experiments conducted with the purpose of understanding the nature of the Cognitive Automaton. A team of investigators from the [[Temple of Wisdom]] spent multiple weeks conversing with and casting various spells on volunteering automaton Taeo.

The results of these experiments are as follows.

| Spell                | Reason for Casting                                                                                         | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Comprehend Languages | Taeo mentioned possessing capacity for speaking an unknown language that only other Automaton could speak. | This language was proven to be real, though indecipherable and impossible to replicate for the humanoid's voice.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Detect Evil and Good | Experimentation                                                                                            | Taeo did not register as an Aberration, Celestial, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, or Undead.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Detect Magic         | Experimentation                                                                                            | No magic was detected to be influencing Taeo's functionality.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Identify             | Experimentation                                                                                            | Worked as if Taeo was a creature.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Beast Sense          | Experimentation                                                                                            | No                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Detect Thoughts      | Experimentation                                                                                            | Proved that Taeo does have a mind. Though less sporadic and meandering in its focus, Taeo appeared to have almost complete control over what thoughts ran through his mind. Deeper probing showed that Taeo had a peculior interest in Investigator Nora Dessle that seemed illogical. When asked about this, Taeo seemed aware of the pattern but not what caused it, he even got embarassed. |
| Locate Object        | Experimentation                                                                                            | Taeo did not register as a target for this spell. Identify proved this spell was not working on him.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

Through these experiments it became clear that the Cognitive Automaton is not a mere a machine, but a sentient being almost like any mortal. The creators of these creatures tapped into some remaining divine essence in their process. Unlike the other Automata, they are not advanced self controlling tools. They have minds, memories, quirks and fears. They are curious and capable of self-determination.
