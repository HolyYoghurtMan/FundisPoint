Refers to the year when the [[Invaders]] first arrived. Little is really known about that time.

The oldest elves, gnomes and dwarves of the city have first hand accounts of their journey. 

We know that they first broke through shortly after the winter festivities had finished and in the southern hemisphere of the planet and razed their path north at a rapid pace.

From a journal written by a servant of a general in the [[Great Empire]] we learned the following:
	The nations of the time organized measly defenses, the first real battle against the Invaders took place some weeks after that. A small, island nation organized their fleet of warships to protect the capital island. 
	Upon receiving the bird that carried news of its destruction, many in the war room fell to despair, but the Emperor did not. He bid his army muster, his populace evacuate and they travelled north. Sending birds to kingdoms to come together and fight this scourge.

Many other kingdoms tried to fight on their own and were wiped away.

The Empire's call to unify was heard. Some letters describe the desperate battle they fought.
- The cathedral fell. My grandad work on the towers when my mother was carrying me. Now the city they called home is ash, and their work rubble, and the fields I played in lined with trenches and craters.
  Every hour the rumbling to the south grows louder. I will die here. I will be flattened by a being that knows no better than to kill, or I will drown in the mud if I set one foot wrong. I never said it enough, darling, but I love you. Only the thought of seeing you and the little one on the other side is what will let me face death proudly.
- A thousand more men upwards into the slaughter. Where do we run to? No wall has stopped the tide yet. No force broke the wave. Even this letter has no point, you will either die before you read it, or you will die after. Goodbye.
- ...

Around the middle of summer a searing white light shone in the north. A beacon shining into the night and a legion of celestials came down to spread the word of safety and repel the invaders.

Some travelers said their travel to this safe haven was magically cut short, a journey that would take months, reduced to but a week's travel.
