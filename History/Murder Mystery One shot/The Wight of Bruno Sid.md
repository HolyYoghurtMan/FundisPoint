Bruno was a kind man who was engaged for his community. When it was anounced he and his coworkers would be fired and replaced with constructs, he was moved to protest.

When the protests turned to riots, guards were engaged to quell them. Bruno was caught between rioters and guards and died.

Bruno's spirit was spurred to anger by the [[butcher of metal]] and rose again. Bruno roams the sewers beneath the city, waiting until his master bids him to strike. And so became corporeal again. The right side of his face is caved in from the blow that killed him.
Bruno can additionally cast Silence and has the Eldritch invocation One With Shadows.
