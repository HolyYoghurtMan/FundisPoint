A rich Dwarven factory owner has been found dead in an alley at midnight. His name is Imvarr Coghorn. He was beaten, and stabbed 15 times. The fingers on his right hand have all been cut off. The Coghorn factory does metalwork, usually for use in the steampipes of their borough. Imvarr’s father created the factory from nothing 300 years ago. They made it big after a deal with the creators of the Telepolos, supplying them with many of the small parts required for the construction of them.

Witnesses:
- [[A homeless orc named Tibar]].
- [[shifty human fella named Peter]]


Imvarr himself will rise as a [[Zombie]] on the coroner's table, as a wight is the one who killed him.

Killer is [[The Wight of Bruno Sid]], a worker who died in a protest turned riot at the Coghorn factory hundreds of years ago. The riot was viciously quelled by the company’s security mercenaries. The [[butcher of metal]] raised this wight.
